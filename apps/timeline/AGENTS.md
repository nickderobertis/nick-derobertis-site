# Timeline rules

This remote publishes the Home timeline pane and its standalone document.

## Journeys

1. The complete CV history renders, the education and employment filters work,
   and a filter with no matches reports itself, through both the standalone
   remote and the host-composed Home page.
2. Its loading, empty, and error states are presented and an invalid timeline
   state is rejected, through both render paths.
3. Compact labels are used at a mobile viewport, and the standalone remote loads
   the shared design-system foundation.
