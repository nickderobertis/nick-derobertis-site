# Home story rules

This remote publishes the Home story pane. It owns the pane's
content, its states, and its layout; Home owns only the composition that mounts
it.

## Journeys

1. The pane's happy path is accessible, its skeleton shows while its boundary is
   pending, and its empty and error states are visible, through both the
   standalone remote and the host-composed Home page.
2. The pane fits the mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints through both render
   paths.
